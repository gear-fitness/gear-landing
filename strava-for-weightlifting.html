The social first lifting app

Strava for weightlifting

Runners kudos a Saturday long run. Lifters still screenshot a notes app. Gear is Strava for weightlifting: a friends feed that updates set by set, with streaks, PRs, and volume on the same surface.

Strava made endurance sport social without turning it into a generic feed. Gear does that for the weight room. The log is real — sets, load, rest — and the people you lift with sit next to it, not in a buried tab.

Friends feed, set by set

When someone hits a set, it can land in the feed. You see the session as it happens, not a recap three days later.

Streaks

Show up, keep the flame. Streaks are visible to the people who actually care that you walked in.

PRs

A personal record is a social event. Gear treats it that way instead of hiding it in a history chart.

Volume

Weekly tonnage is the scoreboard. Not a vanity metric bolted on — the work you already did, readable by friends.

Built for the gym, not the road

This is not a run tracker with a weights tab. Gear: Social Fitness Tracker is an iOS lifting app. If you want the entity definition, read What is Gear?. If you want how that sits against today’s default logger, see the social lifting app and Hevy alternative.
