# Gear Fitness Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 29, 2026

Gear Fitness ("Gear Fitness," "we," "us," or "our") provides a mobile application for logging workouts and nutrition, tracking progress, and sharing training activity with friends (the "App"), together with the website at https://gearfitness.app (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the Service.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account information

When you sign in with Google or Apple we receive:

- Your **email address** (if you use Sign in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email," this is an Apple private-relay address)
- Your **name** (used to suggest a display name)

We do **not** receive or store your Google or Apple password. During onboarding and in Settings you may also provide:

- A **username** and **display name**
- A **profile picture** (uploaded from your photo library or taken with your camera)
- A **private-account setting** (on or off)
- A **home gym**

### 1.2 Body and fitness profile information (optional)

You may choose to provide self-reported physical attributes used to personalize the App:

- **Height**
- **Weight**
- **Age**
- **Gender**

These fields are optional. You can edit them at any time in Settings.

### 1.3 Apple Health data (iOS, optional)

If, and only if, you grant permission through Apple's HealthKit prompt, the App will:

- **Read** your most recent **height** and **weight** samples from Apple Health to pre-fill your profile; and
- **Write** your height and weight back to Apple Health when you change them in the App.

We do not read workouts, heart rate, location, or any other Health data type. Health data handling commitments:

- Health data is used **only** to provide the features described above.
- We **never** use Apple Health data for advertising or marketing.
- We **never** send Apple Health data to our analytics provider.
- We **never** sell Apple Health data or share it with third parties for their own purposes, including data brokers or information resellers.
- You can revoke Health access at any time in iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Gear Fitness**. The App checks the live permission state and stops syncing immediately once access is revoked.

### 1.4 Workout and activity data

When you use the App's core features, we collect the training data you log:

- Workouts (name, date performed, start/end time, duration, and the gym you tag, if any)
- Exercises and sets (reps, weight, ordering, notes), including cardio activities
- Personal records (computed from your sets)
- Routines (exercise templates and scheduled days)
- Rest days, streak counts, and streak-restore usage
- Custom exercises you create
- Workout photos you choose to attach

### 1.5 Nutrition data (optional)

If you use nutrition tracking, we collect:

- **Food logs**: foods, quantities, meals, and the calories and macronutrients recorded for them
- **Saved meals** and **nutrition goals**
- **Barcodes** you scan to look up a product
- **Text descriptions and photos of food** you submit for an AI nutrition estimate. Food photos are used only to produce the estimate and are **deleted from our storage as soon as the estimate is returned**.

### 1.6 Social activity and messages

If you use the social features, we collect:

- **Posts** (a shared workout, optional caption, optional photo) and **@mentions**
- **Likes** and **comments** you make or receive
- **Follow relationships** (who you follow, who follows you, and pending/accepted/declined requests), and gyms you follow
- **Direct and group messages**, including message text, photos, reactions, and group names and images
- **Reports** you submit about content or users, and users you **block**
- **In-app notifications** generated by this activity

If your account is **public**, your profile, posts, and workout summaries are visible to other Gear Fitness users, and public posts and profiles can also be viewed by anyone on the web through a share link (for example, when you share a post outside the App). If your account is **private**, your posts are visible only to followers you have approved and are not shown on share links. Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation. We do not read your messages; they are processed automatically only to store and deliver them.

### 1.7 Location (optional)

If you allow location access, the App uses your device's location, only while you are using the App, to suggest nearby gyms when you pick a home gym or tag a workout. On Android the App requests approximate location only. On iOS, the location may be precise unless you turn off **Precise Location** for Gear Fitness in your device settings. We send these coordinates to our servers and to Google Places only to find nearby gyms. **We do not store the coordinates.** The gym you choose is stored and, like your other profile and workout details, may be visible to other users according to your privacy settings. You can search for a gym by name without granting location access.

### 1.8 Purchases

If you subscribe to Gear Plus, the purchase is processed by Apple. We and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receive your **subscription status and purchase history** (product, dates, renewal, trial, and cancellation status), linked to your account ID. We **never** receive your payment card details.

### 1.9 Usage analytics and diagnostics

We use PostHog, a product analytics provider, to understand how the App is used and to diagnose crashes. When analytics is on, the App sends:

- **Events for actions you take in the App**, for example signing in, completing a workout (its duration and number of exercises and sets), following someone, or logging food (the logging method only). These events **never** include the foods you log, the weights you lift, your body measurements, Apple Health data, message text, or anything else you type.
- The **names of the screens** you view
- **App lifecycle events** (install, open, background, update)
- **Device and app information**: device model, operating system and version, and app version
- **Crash and error reports** (technical error messages and stack traces)
- Your **IP address**, which PostHog uses to infer an **approximate location** (such as city and country)

Once you sign in, these events are linked to your **Gear account ID**, **username**, **subscription tier**, and **account creation date**. We do not send your email address. Once a day our servers also send PostHog **counts** of your followers, the accounts you follow, and your mutual follows. You can turn analytics off at any time (Section 5.1).

### 1.10 Device and technical data

- A **push notification token** (Expo push token) registered for your device, if you enable notifications
- Basic **device and OS information** used to deliver builds and notifications correctly
- **Server logs** generated automatically when the App communicates with our backend (such as IP address, request timestamps, and endpoints accessed), used for security and troubleshooting

The App stores authentication tokens on your device in the secure system keychain (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), not in plain storage.

### 1.11 What we do not collect

- We do **not** use advertising SDKs, and we do **not** track you across other companies' apps or websites.
- We do **not** track your location in the background, and we do **not** store the coordinates used for gym search.
- We do **not** collect your contacts.
- We do **not** sell personal information, and we have not done so in the preceding 12 months.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

1. Create and operate your account and authenticate you (Google sign-in, Apple sign-in, token refresh).
2. Provide the core product: workout and nutrition logging, history, personal-record detection, routines, streaks, gym search, and progress charts.
3. Provide AI features: AI nutrition estimates from text and photos.
4. Provide social features: the feed, profiles, follows, likes, comments, mentions, direct messages, share links, and related notifications.
5. Process and manage Gear Plus subscriptions and free trials.
6. Send **push notifications** you have opted into (for example likes, comments, follow requests, messages, and streak reminders). Notifications are opt-in at the operating-system level and can be turned off at any time.
7. Sync your data across your devices.
8. Understand how the App is used, measure which features work, and find and fix crashes and errors (product analytics and diagnostics).
9. Keep the Service safe: moderate uploaded images and reported content, act on reports, prevent abuse, and maintain security and reliability.
10. Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for targeted advertising, and we do not use your personal data to train AI models.

## 4. Data Retention

- **Account, workout, nutrition, and social data** are retained for as long as your account exists, so the App can show your full history.
- **Content you delete in-app** (workouts and their posts, food logs, comments, custom exercises, routines, profile pictures) is deleted from our production database when you delete it. Workout and post photos are removed from storage when the workout is deleted.
- **Messages** you delete in a conversation are hidden for you only; they remain visible to the other people in the conversation. Messages you sent are deleted when you delete your account.
- **Food photos** submitted for an AI nutrition estimate are deleted as soon as the estimate is returned.
- **Analytics data** and **server logs** are currently retained indefinitely, to support long-term product analysis, security, and troubleshooting. You can ask us to delete analytics data linked to your account at any time (Section 5.3).
- **Backups** of our database may persist for a limited period after deletion before being aged out.
- When you **delete your account** (Section 5.3), we delete your personal data from our production systems, except where we must retain limited records to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.
- If we ever shut down the Service, we will give users at least **90 days' notice** and a way to export their data in a portable format (JSON or CSV).

## 5. Your Choices and Rights

### 5.1 Controls in the App and on your device

- **Private account:** Toggle your account to private in Settings; new followers will then require your approval before they can see your posts.
- **Usage analytics:** Turn off **Settings → Privacy → Share Anonymous Usage Data** (renamed "Share Usage Data" in an upcoming update) to stop the App from sending analytics events from that device. The setting is stored on your device, so turn it off on each device you use. Aggregate follower counts computed on our servers may still be added to your existing analytics profile.
- **Push notifications:** Decline or revoke notification permission in your device's settings, or unregister your device's push token from within the App.
- **Location:** Location access is optional and requested only when you look for nearby gyms. You can revoke it at any time in your device's settings and still search for gyms by name.
- **Apple Health:** Revoke read/write access at any time in iOS Settings (Section 1.3).
- **Camera and photos:** Camera and photo library access are requested only when you take or choose a photo (profile picture, workout or post photo, message photo, or food photo) or save a share card, and can be revoked in device settings.
- **Blocking and reporting:** You can block users and report content or users from within the App.
- **Edit or delete content:** You can edit or delete your workouts, food logs, posts, comments, routines, custom exercises, profile details, and profile picture at any time in the App.

### 5.2 Revoking Google or Apple sign-in access

You can revoke Gear Fitness's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, or to your Apple ID in **Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple** on your iPhone. You will no longer be able to sign in until you grant access again.

### 5.3 Account deletion

You may delete your Gear Fitness account at any time:

- **In the App:** Settings → Privacy → Delete Account; or
- **By email:** Send a request from your registered email address to **support@gearfitness.app** with the subject "Account Deletion."

When you delete your account in the App, it is deactivated immediately. You can restore it by signing back in within **48 hours**; after that, it is permanently purged from our production systems. Deletion removes your profile, workouts, nutrition data, routines, streaks, posts, comments, likes, messages, follow relationships, notifications, and push token. Comments or likes you left on other users' posts are also removed. Your profile picture is deleted from storage. Other photos you uploaded (such as post, workout, and message photos) are no longer accessible through the Service or to other users, but copies may remain in our secure storage. You can ask us to delete them by emailing **support@gearfitness.app**. Deleting your account does **not** automatically delete analytics data (Section 1.9). To have analytics data linked to your account deleted, email **support@gearfitness.app** and we will delete it within 30 days. Once purged, deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We will complete email deletion requests within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Gear Plus subscription billed by Apple. Cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings.

### 5.4 Data export

You may request a copy of your data in a portable format (JSON or CSV) by emailing **support@gearfitness.app**.

### 5.5 Regional privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, California, or other US states with privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To exercise any of these rights, contact **support@gearfitness.app**. We will verify your request using your registered email address and respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

If you are in the EEA or UK, our legal bases for processing are: performance of our contract with you (providing the Service, including subscriptions); your consent (for example, Apple Health access, location, and push notifications), which you can withdraw at any time; and our legitimate interests in keeping the Service secure and reliable, moderating content, and understanding and improving the App through product analytics and crash diagnostics. You can object to analytics at any time by turning it off (Section 5.1). You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

**California and other US state residents:** our service providers, including our analytics provider, process personal information on our behalf and only for the purposes described in this Policy. We do not "sell" personal information or "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined under California law.

## 6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

- HTTPS/TLS encryption for traffic between the App and our servers
- Authentication via short-lived JSON Web Tokens with refresh-token rotation
- Federated sign-in with Google and Apple, so we never collect or store passwords
- Secure on-device token storage in the iOS Keychain / Android Keystore
- Short-lived, signed links for viewing stored photos
- Access controls limiting who on the team can reach production systems

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If we learn of a security breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you as required by applicable law.
