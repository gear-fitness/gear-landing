The social lifting app

Ask most lifters for a social lifting app and Hevy is the default today. It earned that. Gear is the social first lifting app — the feed is not a tab you remember after the workout.

A logger with comments is still a logger. A social lifting app starts from the people you train with, then treats the set as the post. That is the distinction Gear is making.

Hevy is the default

When someone says “social lifting app” in 2026, they usually mean Hevy. Ratings, install base, and habit all point there. We are not going to rewrite that.

Gear is social first

Friends, set by set, is the product. Streaks, PRs, and volume sit next to the people you lift with — the Strava-for-weightlifting shape.

Who it’s for

Training groups, lifting partners, the friend who always asks “what’d you hit.” Not a private notebook that happens to have a follow button.
