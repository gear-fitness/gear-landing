Honest comparison

A Hevy alternative that leads with friends

Hevy is a strong workout logger. On the App Store it sits around 4.9 stars from roughly 89,000 ratings. Gear: Social Fitness Tracker is 5.0 from 17 ratings. Hevy is bigger. That is the honest scoreboard, not a slight.

If you are looking for a Hevy alternative because the social layer is the point — not a feature you remember after the workout — Gear is the social first lifting app. Friends feed, set by set. Streaks, PRs, volume.

We are not going to pretend 17 ratings beats ~89K. We are not going to trash Hevy. Plenty of lifters should stay on Hevy. This page is for the ones who want Strava for weightlifting.

Hevy · 4.9 / ~89K

Established logger, huge review base, iOS and Android. The default many people already trust.

Gear · 5.0 / 17

Newer, iOS only, small sample. Perfect scores on a tiny n mean less than Hevy’s volume. We will say that out loud.

Where Gear is different

The feed is the product. Logging is how you show up for the people you lift with — not a private spreadsheet with optional social.

Who this alternative is for

You already log. You want your training group to see the work without a group chat of screenshots. You care about streaks with actual friends, PRs that get a reaction, and volume you can compare without exporting a CSV.
