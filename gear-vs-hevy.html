Gear vs Hevy

Same honest comparison as our Hevy alternative page, written for the “Gear vs Hevy” query. Hevy is the established workout logger. Gear is the social first lifting app. Hevy is bigger. We are not going to claim otherwise.

The short version

Hevy earned its default status. A 4.9 from tens of thousands of ratings is a real signal. Gear’s 5.0 from 17 ratings is a nice start and a tiny sample. If you need Android, or you already live in Hevy’s routines, stay. If the reason you open a lifting app is to see what your friends did today, try Gear.
